<div class="values-grid">
<div class="value-card"><span>01</span><h3>Start with impact</h3><p>I work backward from the outcome: a better decision, lower cost, faster operation, or stronger customer experience. Technology is the means, never the scorecard.</p></div>
<div class="value-card"><span>02</span><h3>Build for leverage</h3><p>The best systems multiply a team’s ability. I look for platforms, automation, and operating models that make good work easier to repeat at scale.</p></div>
<div class="value-card"><span>03</span><h3>Grow capability</h3><p>A mature data organization does more than answer requests. It teaches the wider business to ask better questions, experiment, and act with confidence.</p></div>
<div class="value-card"><span>04</span><h3>Earn trust by design</h3><p>Quality, reliability, security, governance, and responsible AI belong in the architecture from the beginning—not in a cleanup phase.</p></div>
<div class="value-card"><span>05</span><h3>Experiment with discipline</h3><p>I pair curiosity with measurement: test ideas, define evidence, learn quickly, and scale only what proves useful.</p></div>
<div class="value-card"><span>06</span><h3>Practice the craft</h3><p>I care about the details beneath durable outcomes. Craftsmanship, resilience, and a growth mindset are how ambitious systems survive contact with reality.</p></div>
